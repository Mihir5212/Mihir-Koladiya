Engineering Profile

Bridging highway engineering and spatial research

A transportation engineer with a dual focus on field practice and academic research — road safety, crash hotspot identification, and geospatial analysis of infrastructure risk.

With an M.E. in Highway & Transportation Engineering from MSU Baroda, and currently a Project Engineer at SVNIT Surat, I apply QGIS, ArcGIS, Python, and AI/ML methods to analyse crash patterns, identify blackspots, and support data-driven highway safety decisions.
