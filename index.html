How I work

From crash records to safer roads

A four-step, evidence-based approach I apply on highway safety projects.

  1. 01

    Collect & clean

    Crash data cleaning, frequency and severity analysis, and contributing-factor identification.

    Python · SPSS · Jamovi
  2. 02

    Map hotspots

    Crash mapping, KDE-based heatmaps, spatial clustering and corridor-level safety interpretation.

    QGIS · ArcGIS · Earth Engine
  3. 03

    Model the risk

    Count models (Poisson, Negative Binomial, Zero-Inflated, Hurdle), GWR, and AI/ML with XGBoost, Random Forest and ANN.

    R · Python · XGBoost
  4. 04

    Audit & recommend

    Road safety audits, field validation and IRC-guided countermeasures for blackspot treatment.

    IRC:SP-88 · IRC:SP-84 · AutoCAD
