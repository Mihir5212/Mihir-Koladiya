Engineering Profile

Bridging highway engineering and spatial research

A transportation engineer with a dual focus in field practice and academic research. My work centres on road safety, crash hotspot identification, and geospatial analysis of transport infrastructure risk — with the goal of informing evidence-based engineering decisions.

Trained at SVNIT Surat with an M.Tech in Transportation Engineering, I apply spatial tools such as ArcGIS, QGIS, and Python to analyse crash patterns, evaluate highway corridors, and develop GIS-integrated safety frameworks.
